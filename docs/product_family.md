# PRISM Product-Family Contract

PRISM is part of the same visual product family as The Palm and NEXUS.

Future UI work should preserve the shared foundation rather than introduce a separate product palette. The canonical dark foundation is `#07110d`, with `#78e6aa` as the primary green and `#c8f56b` as the limited lime highlight. Enterprise surfaces should remain within the `#0b1712` / `#0e1d17` range, with borders near `#1e3229` and muted copy near `#8fa39a`.

Product-specific identity should come from information architecture, iconography and function—not from changing the core color system.
